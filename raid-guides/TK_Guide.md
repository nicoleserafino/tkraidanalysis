## 🔥 Al'ar  (Lust: ON PULL, ~12s)
Two-platform phoenix. **Lust immediately** to burn Phase 1.
- **Phase 1:** Al'ar flies between platforms. Off-tank must be on the destination platform BEFORE it lands so it doesn't dive-bomb the raid. It does NOT melee normally — it's about positioning.
- **Dive Bombs:** it flies to a random spot and slams — get out of the fire patches it leaves.
- **Phase 2 (~starts when P1 down):** Al'ar crashes to center. **Meteor** spawns **Ember of Al'ar** adds — pick them up fast (they explode and heal Al'ar if they reach it). Flame Patches + Flame Buffet on the tank.
- **Callouts:** "Tank get to the next platform!" • "Kill the Embers, don't let them reach Al'ar!" • "Spread — dive bomb!" • Movement fight, casters expect low uptime.

## ⚡ Void Reaver  (Lust: ON PULL, ~12s)  — Tanks: **Mightystorm** + **Furboll**
Pure execution / "vibe" fight. **Lust on pull.**
- **Arcane Orb:** VR launches orbs at ranged; **they hit the ground and explode for huge damage.** Ranged must **spread and physically dodge** the orb's landing spot. This is the #1 killer — standing in an orb = dead.
- **Pounding:** raid-wide AoE every ~12s (~1250 dmg x2). Healers heal through it; **it also knocks off totems** — shamans re-drop.
- **Knock Away:** wipes the main tank's threat → **OT (Furboll) taunts** immediately, MT rebuilds.
- **Callouts:** "SPREAD for orbs!" • "Dodge the orb!" • "Pounding — heal up." • "Taunt on Knock Away." Melee stack behind; ranged spread ~10yd.

## 🌌 High Astromancer Solarian  (Lust: ON PULL, ~13s)  — Tank: **Furboll**
Three phases. **Lust on pull** to push P1 fast.
- **Phase 1:** Solarian + adds. She splits into portals spawning **Solarium Agents** (melee — kill, ~12 of them) & **Solarium Priests** (**interrupt/kill — they heal + Psychic Scream fear**).
- **Wrath of the Astromancer (the BOMB):** she marks a player with a debuff that **explodes** after ~6s. **Marked player RUNS 30+ yards away from everyone** or it chains and wipes clumps. Biggest wipe cause here.
- **Phase 3 (~20% HP):** she transforms into a **giant Void Walker** — hits much harder, tank cooldowns up, just burn.
- **Callouts:** "Bomb on <name> — RUN OUT!" • "Interrupt the Priests!" • "Kill Agents." • "Phase 3 — tank CDs, burn her down."

## 👑 Kael'thas Sunstrider  (Lust: DELAYED → ~307s / Phase 5)  — long fight (~8 min)
Marathon fight, multiple phases. **Do NOT lust early — save it for the Gravity Lapse / final burn (~5 min in).**
- **Phase 2 — Advisors (one at a time):**
  - **Thaladred the Darkener** — fixates/gazes a random player (chases them); tank picks up when he resets. Runner kites.
  - **Lord Sanguinar** — Fear; tank fast.
  - **Grand Astromancer Capernian** — ranged caster, **stays at range, casts Fireball/Conflagrate.** **Warlock tanks her** (Kamiguruu / Guaaripolo per your setup) since she won't come to melee.
  - **Master Engineer Telonicus** — Remote Toy + Bomb; tank it.

  _Tuesday order & tanks: Thaladred (Furboll picks up after fixate) → Sanguinar → **Capernian (Kamiguruu warlock-tanks her at range)** → Telonicus._
- **Phase 3 — Weapons:** 7 legendary weapons drop and attack. **Assign players to each weapon** by threat; they despawn after a bit. (This is the phase your fight 41 attempt bugged on before — make sure interrupts/mechanics are firing.)
- **Phase 4 — Kael solo:** Fireball (**interrupt/kick**), Mind Control (~3-4 players — CC/off them or wait it out), Flamestrike (get out of the fire), Arcane Disruption (silence). **He also summons a Phoenix — kill it, then kill the Phoenix Egg it leaves before it rehatches.**
- **Phase 5 — Gravity Lapse:** everyone floats into the air, Kael nukes. **THIS is where you Lust (~307s Tuesday).** Nether Vapor + Shock Barrier — burn him while airborne.
- **Callouts:** "Warlock on Capernian." • "Weapon assignments — grab yours." • "KICK the Fireball." • "Kill the Phoenix + EGG." • "Mind Control — CC/stop DPS on <name>." • "Gravity Lapse — LUST, burn!" • "Flamestrike, move out."
